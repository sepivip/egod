The suspension lab

A dirt bike rides a rolling test lane in the middle of a workshop. Everything in the room changes how it handles. Break it, fix it, and watch why.

  • Look aroundDrag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Touch thingsClick the glowing dots: spring rack, control desk, rider scale, track board, drop hoist.
  • Read the gaugesGold is the fork, blue is the rear shock. Red means it hit the end of its travel.
