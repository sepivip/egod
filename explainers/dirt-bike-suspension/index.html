The suspension lab

A dirt bike rides a rolling test lane in the middle of a workshop. Everything in the room changes how it handles. Break it, fix it, and watch why.

  • Look aroundDrag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Touch thingsClick the fork or the shock on the bike to tune it, or a glowing dot for the spring rack, scale, track board and hoist.
  • Set the sagWeigh the rider on the scale, then turn the shock preload until both sag bars are green.
  • Read the gaugesGold is the fork, blue is the shock. Red means it hit the end of its travel.
